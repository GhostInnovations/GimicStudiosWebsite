Music

Gimic Studios music division includes full recording studio, photography studio, editing suite, suite and record label

Gimic Records is an independent record label and publishing house specialising in producing local talent in a multitude of music genres. Most of our releases are available on mainstream online stores such as iTunes, Amazon, Google play, Youtube Music etc. but to support musicians we would recommend you either buy a physical CD from us or download from the artists Bandcamp pages.

We also provide sound editing and film score services such as Foley, effects, partial and full score services

If you are interested in hiring our team or recording studio for your project or if you would like to send us your Demo, for consideration please drop us an email at: studiogimic@gmail.com
